Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-27

Film Cost Calculator is designed to run with no advertising trackers and no user profiling. This page explains what is stored and why.

1. Tracking and Analytics

  • No third-party analytics scripts are used.
  • No advertising or behavioral tracking tools are used.
  • No fingerprinting features are intentionally implemented.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

  • This app does not require non-essential cookies.
  • Calculator entries and UI state are stored in your browser local storage so your data persists between visits.
  • Your privacy consent preference is stored locally in your browser.

3. Data Collection

  • No account is required.
  • No personal data is intentionally collected by the app itself.
  • When brand logos are displayed, your browser may request image assets from a logo CDN (`logo.clearbit.com`).
  • Typical server logs may exist at the infrastructure level (for security and uptime), managed by the site operator.

4. Your Choices

  • You can clear local storage at any time in your browser settings.
  • You can reject non-essential cookies in the consent prompt (none are used by default).

5. Contact

For policy questions, contact the site maintainer via the project repository.
